About inSileco.io
Getting started

Welcome to inSileco

Let's take a quick tour of your workspace so you know where everything lives. It only takes a few seconds — you can skip anytime and replay it later from your account settings.

Projects

Your work is organised into projects. Each project gathers the datasets, reports, automations and apps for a single body of work. Open a project card to dive in.

Datasets & reports

Datasets are your raw and processed data — the single source of truth. Reports turn that data into shareable, versioned documents you can hand to your team or stakeholders.

Automations & apps

Automations run your data workflows on a schedule or on demand. Apps are interactive tools — dashboards, maps and forms — that let anyone explore the results without touching code.
