What are reports?

Shareable, generated documents

Reports are shareable documents summarising findings from your data. They combine narrative, figures and tables produced from one or more datasets, and are versioned so you can always trace which dataset version produced which conclusion. A report can be a draft while in progress, then move to published when it is ready to distribute outside the team.
