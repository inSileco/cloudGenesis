What are applications?

Interactive tools built on your project data

Applications are browser-based, interactive tools built on top of your project — dashboards, map explorers, data-entry forms and custom visualisations designed for non-technical users. They let stakeholders explore data, make decisions and contribute observations without touching code, and they stay in sync with the underlying datasets through the project's automations.
