What are automations?

Scheduled or on-demand workflows

Automations are workflows that move and transform data without manual intervention — ETL pipelines, model runs, tile generators, anything that should happen repeatedly on a schedule or on demand. Each automation has a defined sequence of steps, a schedule, and a run history with success or failure status. They are how raw datasets become the polished inputs that reports and apps depend on.
